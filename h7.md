## **Summary of Readings**
### **Quintin 2014: 7 Things You Should Know About Tor**
- **TOR (The Onion Router)** is a tool for anonymous browsing.
- It hides identity by sending data through **multiple encrypted nodes**.
- It is **not a VPN**—only traffic inside the TOR network is protected.
- Used by **journalists, activists, and privacy-conscious users**.
- Some sites **block TOR users**.
- Governments and law enforcement have **methods to track TOR users**.

### **Shavers & Bair 2016: Hiding Behind the Keyboard**
#### **Introduction**
- Discusses how **cybercriminals** use TOR for illegal activities.
- TOR is also used for **legal privacy reasons**.

#### **History and Intended Use of TOR**
- Created by the **U.S. Navy** for secure communication.
- Now used by **many people who want privacy**.

#### **How TOR Works**
- Uses **onion routing** (multiple encryption layers).
- **Entry node** knows your IP, but **exit node** does not.
- Makes tracking difficult, but not **impossible**.

#### **Tracking Criminals Using TOR**
- Authorities use **hacking, traffic analysis, and undercover operations**.
- TOR users need **good OPSEC (Operational Security)** to stay anonymous.
